# Mission Statement

Deliver a single, governed lakehouse that eliminates data duplication, empowers every consumer — from BI analysts to AI models — to query one authoritative copy of data, and gives the organization full control of its data destiny, independent of any single vendor or compute platform.

## Why This Matters

Our financial services organization manages 1.5 PB of data across 300+ sources. Today, the same data is copied across Teradata, Cloudera, and Snowflake — creating cost, complexity, inconsistency, and risk. This project exists to end that cycle.

## What Success Looks Like

- **One copy of truth** — every dataset lives in Apache Iceberg, accessible by any engine, with no redundant copies.
- **Any consumer, any workload** — BI dashboards, SQL analysts, AI/ML pipelines, and regulatory reports all query the same governed data.
- **Vendor independence** — open formats, open catalogs, open protocols. We control our data, not a vendor's proprietary platform.
- **Enterprise trust** — security, lineage, quality, and governance are built in from day one, not bolted on after the fact.
